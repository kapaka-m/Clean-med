UNDERSTANDING THE SCORE

Coverage, expressed simply.

Every symptom has equal weight. We divide selected symptoms from a condition’s rule by the number of symptoms in that rule.

Selected rule symptoms
Total rule symptoms× 100
3 of 5 symptoms = 60% matchNot a 60% chance of having a condition.

What the engine can — and can’t — tell you

Transparent by intention

Results sort by coverage, then the number of matching symptoms, then condition name. Unselected symptoms are unknown, not confirmed absent. Additional symptoms are shown and do not reduce a score.

A deliberately bounded model

No trained model, Bayesian probability, medical history, symptom severity, prevalence, or multi-step inference is involved. Several conditions can match the same selection. Even a 100% rule match is not a diagnosis.

Privacy in practice

The application makes no API calls and stores no symptom history. Selections live in memory until the page reloads. Only your display theme is saved locally. Exporting creates a file on your device.
